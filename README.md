**Project Overview**
The HR & Employee Performance Dashboard is an interactive Power BI dashboard that provides insights into workforce data, employee performance, and departmental productivity. It enables HR teams and management to make data-driven decisions regarding employee evaluation, engagement, and organizational growth.

This dashboard is designed to transform raw HR data into meaningful KPIs, visualizations, and trends for better workforce management.

**Features**

📊 Employee Performance Analysis – Track individual and departmental performance.

🏢 Department Productivity – Compare productivity across different departments.

📅 Attendance & Work Hours – Monitor employee attendance trends.

💡 KPI Metrics – Performance rating, average productivity, attrition rate, and more.

📈 Interactive Visuals – Dynamic charts, filters, and drill-down analysis.


**Technologies Used**

Power BI (Dashboard & Data Visualization)
Excel / CSV Data Sources (HR dataset)

**Use Cases**

HR Managers can monitor employee productivity.

Companies can track attrition, performance, and department growth.

Useful for annual performance reviews and HR decision-making.
